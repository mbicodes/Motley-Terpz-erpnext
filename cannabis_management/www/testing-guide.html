Testing Guide — Sales Privacy, Quote Approvals & Orders

A plain-English walkthrough for checking that the new CRM features work. No technical knowledge needed — just log in, follow the steps, and confirm you see what's described. Each test takes about a minute.

Where: your CRM at erp.alltechvirtual.com Who this is for: Operations / implementation tester Sample data: already created for you

00 Before you start

How to "become" another staff member (no password needed)

Most tests ask you to check what a specific person sees. You can switch into their account safely:

  1. Log in as an administrator, then open erp.alltechvirtual.com/app/user
  2. Click the person's name in the list.
  3. Top-right ⋯ menu → "Login As".
  4. Do the test. When finished, log out and log back in as yourself.

Tip: if a button or page looks missing, refresh with Shift + reload once. If it's still missing, let your developer know before continuing.

01 Who can see what

These checks confirm that each person only sees the information they're supposed to — reps can't see each other's customers, warehouse staff can't see money, and Finance can see all the numbers.

A sales rep sees only their own customers & deals

sean@motleyterpz.com
What it does

Sales reps cannot see each other's pipeline — not in lists, boards, search or reports.

How to test
  1. Login As sean@motleyterpz.com.
  2. Open the CRM and click Leads, then Deals.
What you should see

Only Sean's own records. Other reps' leads and deals are nowhere to be found.

The owner sees everything

matt@motleyterpz.com
What it does

Only the owner (Matt) and the system administrator can see across all reps.

How to test
  1. Login As matt@motleyterpz.com.
  2. Open Leads and Deals.
What you should see

Every rep's leads and deals — the full company pipeline.

Finance sees all the money — but not other reps' deals

muhammad.huzaifa@…
What it does

Finance needs the full financial picture, but doesn't need everyone's sales pipeline.

How to test
  1. Login As muhammad.huzaifa@alltechvirtual.com.
  2. Open the money dashboards under Sales Intelligence — Command Center, Cash Projection, AR Aging.
  3. Then open Deals.
What you should see

The money dashboards show company-wide numbers (all customers). The Deals list shows only his own (likely empty) — he can't browse other reps' deals.

Operations cannot see money or pricing

qa_ops_probe@example.com
What it does

Warehouse / fulfillment staff can work on deliveries but must not see balances, revenue or pricing.

How to test
  1. Login As qa_ops_probe@example.com.
  2. Open the Command Center and other money dashboards.
  3. Try opening a customer's financial page.
What you should see

All money/AR figures show as zero or blank, and the customer financial page says "Not permitted." Delivery-related information is still visible.

02 Quotes, approvals & orders

These checks confirm that discounts route to the right approver, quotes can be approved or rejected in one click, and an approved quote becomes an order.

Discounts decide who must approve a quote

View only
What it does

Small discounts go through automatically; bigger ones need sign-off. Under 10% is automatic, 10–20% needs a Sales Manager, 20% or more needs Finance.

How to test
  1. Open each sample quote and scroll to the "Quote Approval" section:
  2. SAL-QTN-2026-00015 (5%), …00016 (15%), …00017 (25%).
What you should see

00015 → Approved automatically · 00016 → needed Sales Manager · 00017 → needs Finance. The "Effective Discount %" matches the discount.

Overdue customers always go to Finance

View only
What it does

If a customer has any unpaid, past-due invoice, their quotes go to Finance no matter how small the discount.

How to test
  1. Open SAL-QTN-2026-00018 — AB Customer, only 3% off.
What you should see

Still "Waiting for Finance" — because this customer is past due — even though 3% would normally be automatic.

Approve or reject a quote

Finance / Sales Manager
What it does

The right approver can approve or reject in one click, from the quote itself or from the Approvals list.

How to test
  1. Login As Finance (muhammad.huzaifa@…).
  2. Open SAL-QTN-2026-00017 → click Approve Quotation or Reject Quotation at the top.
  3. If you reject, you must type a reason.
What you should see

The status updates immediately, and the rep who made the quote gets notified. A rejected quote won't submit until fixed.

A Sales Manager cannot approve the biggest discounts

douglas@motleyterpz.com
What it does

Sales Managers can clear up to 20%. Anything higher (or overdue-customer quotes) is Finance-only.

How to test
  1. Login As douglas@motleyterpz.com.
  2. Open SAL-QTN-2026-00017 (25%, Finance-level).
What you should see

No Approve button for Douglas on this quote — only Finance can approve it.

The Approvals page (now right under "Deals")

Finance / any approver
What it does

A single list of every quote waiting for you, with Approve / Reject built in.

How to test
  1. Login As Finance → in the left menu click Approvals (directly under Deals).
  2. Approve or reject a quote from the list (rejecting asks for a reason).
  3. Then Login As a rep (sean@…) and open the same Approvals page.
What you should see

Finance sees the waiting quotes. The rep sees "All caught up" (reps don't approve anything).

The rejection reason is recorded on the deal

View only
What it does

When a quote is rejected, the reason is saved on the linked deal so everyone can see why.

How to test
  1. Open deal CRM-DEAL-2026-00007 and look at its Activity / timeline.
What you should see

A note saying quote …00019 was rejected, with the reason ("margin too thin").

Turn an approved quote into an order — one click

Any staff
What it does

An approved quote becomes a Sales Order without re-typing anything; the quote, order and deal stay linked.

How to test
  1. Open approved quote SAL-QTN-2026-00015 → click Create Sales Order.
  2. Or open the finished order SAL-ORD-2026-00199 to see the result.
What you should see

An order for the same customer, with each line linked back to the quote, and the deal reference carried across.

See live stock while building a quote

Any staff
What it does

Reps see how much of each product is actually available, so they don't quote something that isn't in stock.

How to test
  1. Start a new quotation.
  2. Choose a Price List, then add an item.
  3. Click Check Stock Availability at the top.
What you should see

The item list is limited to products in that price list, an "Avail. Qty" column shows current stock, and the button shows stock per warehouse.

Daily reminders for overdue invoices

Automatic · no blocking
What it does

Every morning, the rep responsible for a customer gets an email listing that customer's unpaid, past-due invoices — repeated daily until it's paid. Nothing is blocked; it's a nudge, not a lock.

How to test
  1. This runs automatically each morning — confirm reps start receiving the daily email.
  2. To see the current list on demand, ask your developer to run the built-in "preview."
What you should see

Each rep receives one email listing their overdue customers, invoice amounts and due dates. The reminder stops on its own once an invoice is paid.

If something doesn't match

Note three things and send them over: (1) which test, (2) which account you were logged in as, (3) what you expected vs. what you actually saw. A screenshot helps.
