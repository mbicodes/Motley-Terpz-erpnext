Testing Guide — Sales Privacy, Quote Approvals & Orders

A plain-English walkthrough for checking that the new CRM features work. No technical knowledge needed — just log in, follow the steps, and confirm you see what's described. Each test takes about a minute.

Where: your CRM at erp.alltechvirtual.com Who this is for: Operations / implementation tester Sample data: already created for you

00 Before you start

How to "become" another staff member (no password needed)

Most tests ask you to check what a specific person sees. You can switch into their account safely:

  1. Log in as an administrator, then open erp.alltechvirtual.com/app/user
  2. Click the person's name in the list.
  3. Top-right ⋯ menu → "Login As".
  4. Do the test. When finished, log out and log back in as yourself.

Tip: if a button or page looks missing, refresh with Shift + reload once. If it's still missing, let your developer know before continuing.

01 Who can see what

These checks confirm that each person only sees the information they're supposed to — reps can't see each other's customers, warehouse staff can't see money, and Finance can see all the numbers.

A sales rep sees only their own customers & deals

sean@motleyterpz.com

Sales reps cannot see each other's pipeline — not in lists, boards, search or reports.

How to test
  1. Login As sean@motleyterpz.com.
  2. Open the CRM and click Leads, then Deals.
What you should see

Only Sean's own records. Other reps' leads and deals are nowhere to be found.

Daily reminders for overdue invoices

Automatic · no blocking
What it does

Every morning, the rep responsible for a customer gets an email listing that customer's unpaid, past-due invoices — repeated daily until it's paid. Nothing is blocked; it's a nudge, not a lock.

How to test

What you should see

03 Calculators

Two calculators live in the CRM sidebar under Calculators. They help reps price jobs during a conversation. Finance can set the default rates once at /app/co-pack-settings (labor $/hr, overhead %, target margin %).

Co-Pack Calculator

Any staff
What it does

Works out what a co-packing job costs and a suggested price to quote the client, pulling packaging costs live from your item list.

How to test
  1. In the CRM, open Calculators → Co-Pack Calculator.
  2. Enter a raw-material cost, run quantity (e.g. 100), labor hours, and add a packaging item by its code.
  3. Press Calculate.
What you should see

Total cost, cost per unit, and a suggested quote per unit/total (based on the target margin), plus a line-by-line breakdown. Packaging cost = the item's cost × qty × run size.

Tolling Calculator

Any staff
What it does

Works out the per-batch cost of a processing/tolling job from input weight and expected yield.

How to test
  1. Open Calculators → Tolling Calculator.
  2. Enter input pounds (e.g. 100), expected yield % (it pre-fills your historical average), and a tolling rate per lb.
  3. Press Calculate.
What you should see

Expected output weight, total tolling cost, and cost per output-lb. Example: 100 lb × 15% = 15 lb out; $50/input-lb → $5,000 total → $333.33 per output-lb.

04 Leadership dashboards

New reporting pages in the CRM sidebar under Sales Intelligence. The leaderboard is for leadership; the others help everyone stay on top of deliveries and slipping accounts.

Rep Leaderboard

matt / admin only
What it does

Every rep side by side — deals won/lost, win rate, average deal size, open pipeline, revenue and AR owned. Only the owner (Matt) and admin can see it.

How to test
  1. Login As matt@motleyterpz.com → Sales Intelligence → Rep Leaderboard.
  2. Click a column header to sort; change the date range at the top and press Apply.
What you should see

One row per rep with their numbers; totals across the top. Note: Revenue defaults to this month — if your sample invoices are older it may show $0; widen the date range and it fills in.

Goal vs Actual

View
What it does

Opens your existing Sales Target dashboard inside the CRM. It reads targets live from the system, so on the live server it uses that server's targets automatically.

How to test
  1. CRM → Sales Intelligence → Goal vs Actual. Confirm targets and actuals show for the current period.
What you should see

The sales-target dashboard with pro-rated targets vs actual revenue — no hardcoded numbers.

Upcoming Deliveries

Ops + leadership
What it does

Every order with a delivery date that hasn't fully shipped, soonest first, with past-due ones flagged red. Order values are hidden for Operations users.

How to test
  1. CRM → Sales Intelligence → Upcoming Deliveries. Check that overdue rows are red and the count matches the header.
What you should see

A date-sorted list; past-due rows highlighted with a "X days late" badge.

Purchase Insights

Reps see own · leaders see all
What it does

Two lists: customers slowing down (ordering less / spending less), and customers due to reorder based on their own ordering rhythm.

How to test
  1. CRM → Sales Intelligence → Purchase Insights. Read the reasons shown on each account card.
What you should see

Cards with plain-English reasons, e.g. "ordering less often (14→1 in 90d), spend down 93%" or "due to reorder — 175d past their usual cadence."

05 Compliance & accounts

License tracking, sample logging, and the account snapshot's AR aging.

License / compliance warning

View
What it does

Each customer now stores a license number, type and expiry. When a quote or order is built for a customer whose license is expired or expiring within 30 days, a warning pops up (it does not block — just alerts).

How to test
  1. Open customer Expando Products, LLC → the "License / Compliance" section shows an expired license.
  2. Start a new Quotation for that customer and save → a red "License Expired" warning appears.
  3. Try Ben (expiring soon) → an orange "Expiring Soon" warning. A customer with a valid license shows nothing.
What you should see

The right warning for expired / expiring, and no warning for valid or unset licenses.

AR aging on the account snapshot

View
